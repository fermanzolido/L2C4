## 2026-06-04 - Entity Collection Optimization: LinkedList to ArrayList
**Learning:** Found a widespread use of `LinkedList` in high-frequency paths like `World.getVisibleObjects` and skill `TargetHandlers`. In L2J Mobius, these collections are typically populated once and then iterated, making `ArrayList` significantly more efficient due to cache locality and lower object overhead. A specific edge case was found in `Skill.java` where an `instanceof LinkedList` check was used as a safety guard before calling `removeIf`; this must be updated to `ArrayList` when optimizing handlers to prevent logic bypass.
**Action:** Prefer `ArrayList` for all temporary entity collections. When optimizing existing handlers, always check `Skill.getTargetList` for downstream `instanceof` checks that rely on the specific list implementation.
