## 2026-07-04 - Hot Path Collection Refactor: LinkedList vs ArrayList
**Learning:** `LinkedList` is a significant performance anti-pattern in high-frequency visibility and NPC retrieval paths due to lack of CPU cache locality and higher memory overhead. Replacing it with `ArrayList` (with pre-allocated capacity where possible) reduces GC pressure and improves iteration speed. However, one must be extremely careful with logic that performs `instanceof LinkedList` checks for mutability before operations like `removeIf()`, as seen in `Skill.getTargetList()`.
**Action:** Always prioritize `ArrayList` for collection results in model hot paths. When refactoring away from `LinkedList`, grep the codebase for `instanceof LinkedList` to ensure that dependent filtering logic is updated to support the new list type, preventing logic regressions (e.g., monsters buffing playables).
